Topic 1 · The Problem

The "Due Date Illusion" & Unequal Splits

Why traditional student productivity and calendar apps fail.

📅 The Due Date Illusion
Calendars only display the final deadline (e.g. "Friday at 11:59 PM"). On Monday, that feels safely far away—lulling students into a false sense of security until panic sets in Thursday night.
⏳ Estimation Blindness
Students know an assignment requires 10+ hours of focused research, but humans struggle to translate study hours into calendar start days without manual mental math.
👥 The Freeloader & Burnout Trap
Group projects split tasks blindly without factoring in each teammate's real weekly capacity. One member gets overloaded while others take trivial tasks, causing friction and missed deadlines.
💡 The Opportunity: A tool that works backwards from the due date, tells students exactly which day to start, and distributes group tasks according to available weekly hours.
Topic 2 · The Solution

The Start-By Engine & Visual Buffer Bar

A deterministic algorithm that replaces anxiety with calm, actionable start dates.

🧮 The Start-By Math
Assuming a baseline of 2 focused study hours/day, the app applies priority-scaled safety cushions:
  • Low Priority: 1.1× multiplier
  • Medium Priority: 1.3× multiplier
  • High Priority: 1.6× multiplier (extra cushion for high stakes)
const bufferDays = Math.ceil((hours / 2) * multiplier);
startDate.setDate(deadlineDate.getDate() - bufferDays);

Topic 4 · The Challenges

Engineering Roadblocks & How I Solved Them

Four real-world bugs caught, debugged, and resolved during development.

🐛 1. The UTC Timezone Shift Bug
The Problem: Formatting dates via toISOString().split('T')[0] converted to UTC first. For users ahead of UTC (UTC+8), evening dates shifted back a calendar day (Monday showed as Sunday).
The Fix: Discarded UTC formatting and wrote toLocalIsoDate() explicitly reading local clock getFullYear(), getMonth() + 1, and getDate().
🐛 2. Mobile White Screen Crash
The Problem: Desktop Chrome cached older scripts, but visiting on a real phone produced an immediate blank white screen with no error message.
The Fix: Connected Chrome remote mobile debugging and caught ReferenceError: useEffect is not defined in Login.jsx. Restored React hook imports and declared loading states.
🐛 3. Typewriter Layout Shift (CLS)
The Problem: As the hero typewriter animated words of varying length, the text container collapsed to zero height, causing the entire page below to jitter violently.
The Fix: Locked container with min-h-[1.2em], block layout, and inserted a zero-width space (\u200B) so the baseline never collapses.
🐛 4. PostgreSQL RLS Infinite Recursion
The Problem: Writing a member read policy with an EXISTS subquery on project_members inside a policy for project_members crashed Supabase with circular recursion.
The Fix: Broke circular dependency by table aliasing (as pm) and scoping queries directly through project foreign keys.
